### Business Growth Analysis

1.  **Overall Growth Analysis:** High-level summary of business growth over the entire period.
2.  **Monthly KPI Analysis:** Key performance indicators tracked on a monthly basis.
3.  **Quarterly KPI Analysis:** Key performance indicators tracked on a quarterly basis.
4.  **Weekly KPI Analysis:** Key performance indicators tracked on a weekly basis.
5.  **Product Analysis (Monthly, Weekly, Quarterly):** A breakdown of product performance over different timeframes.
6.  **Other Parameters:** Other relevant growth metrics.
