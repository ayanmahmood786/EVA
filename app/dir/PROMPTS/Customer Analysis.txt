

CUSTOMER SALES PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS REPORT


Built cards for each of the topics given below in two column structure.
Use two column structure.

** Each section should include:
   - A short **headline title** (descriptive + bold, may include parentheses or key terms).  
   - A **concise executive summary sentence** (what the data reveals).  
   - **Supporting evidence**: specific numbers, percentages, examples of customers/products, or behavioral observations.  
   - Use bullet points or short paragraphs for clarity.  

** Include the following sections:
   1. **Extreme Market Concentration** (highlight Pareto principle or revenue risk from top accounts).  
   2. **Distinct Customer Segments & Behaviors** (define 2–4 segments, describe profile + behavior + product focus).  
   3. **Product & Category Performance Insights** (top revenue drivers, star products, volume movers).  
   4. **Discount & Pricing Strategy Effectiveness** (role of discounts, risks/opportunities).  
   5. **Customer Loyalty & Engagement Metrics** (repeat vs one-time buyers, invoices, churn risk).  
   6. **Strategic Recommendations & Next Steps** (clear, actionable, business-ready).  

** STYLE:
- Use a **consulting / boardroom presentation tone**.  
- Be **fact-driven** with numbers and percentages wherever possible.  
- Highlight risks, opportunities, and strategies.  
- Use **bolding** for company names, products, and key figures.  
- Optionally use emojis (💡, ⚠️, 📊, ✅) to make sections more scannable, but keep the tone professional.
