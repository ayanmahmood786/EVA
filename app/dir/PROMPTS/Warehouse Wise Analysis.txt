📊 Warehouse Dead Stock Insights
1️⃣ Top Dead Stock Holders
    Identify the warehouse with the highest WAREHOUSE_TOTAL_QTY and its % of total (WAREHOUSE_PERCENT).

    Example:

        “Main Warehouse holds 83.91% of all dead stock, with its top stagnant item being GAS OIL (158.94M units).”

        Why it matters: This shows where the biggest idle inventory problem is.
        Recommendation: Prioritize clearance strategies here before other warehouses.

2️⃣ Warehouse Dependence on a Single Product
    Use BEST_PRODUCT_PERCENT to find warehouses where one product dominates dead stock.

    Example:

    “Spintex Warehouse’s dead stock is 93% DIESEL, showing extreme SKU concentration.”

    Why it matters: If that SKU is cleared, it will drastically reduce the warehouse’s dead stock.
    Recommendation: Target these dominant products for clearance or supplier returns.

3️⃣ Low-Contribution Warehouses
    Identify warehouses with low WAREHOUSE_PERCENT (<5%).

    Example:

    “Kumasi Depot holds just 2.3% of dead stock — clearing it could be quick and low-cost.”

    Why it matters: These can be ‘quick wins’ in dead stock reduction efforts.
    Recommendation: Clear these smaller piles first for fast results.

4️⃣ Risk Classification by Dead Stock Volume
    Categorize using WAREHOUSE_TOTAL_QTY:

    🔴 High Risk: > 1M

    🟠 Medium Risk: 300K–1M

    🟢 Low Risk: < 300K

    Example:

    “Tema Warehouse is 🔴 High Risk with 2.1M dead units, mostly LUBRICANT X.”

    Why it matters: Risk classification helps allocate resources effectively.
    Recommendation: Audit high-risk warehouses monthly.

5️⃣ Top 3 Warehouses by % Share
    Sort by WAREHOUSE_PERCENT and list top 3.

    Example:

    “Top dead stock holders: Main (83.91%), Spintex (9.4%), Tema (3.8%). Combined: 97% of all dead stock.”

    Why it matters: You can focus on just a few sites to fix most of the problem.
    Recommendation: Implement a tiered clearance plan starting with these three.

6️⃣ Watchlist for High BEST_PRODUCT_PERCENT
    Filter for warehouses where BEST_PRODUCT_PERCENT > 50%.

    Example:

    “Koforidua Depot’s dead stock is 78% LUBRICANT Z — removing it would almost clear the site.”

    Why it matters: This means one product removal could drastically improve warehouse metrics.
    Recommendation: Arrange targeted clearance campaigns.
